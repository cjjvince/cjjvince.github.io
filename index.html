SPACE PHYSICS · PLANETARY SCIENCE

Understanding planetary
space environments.

I study how planetary magnetospheres and upper atmospheres interact, from Earth to Jupiter.

I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Earth and Planetary Sciences at The University of Hong Kong and a faculty researcher in the Planetary Space Environment Modeling (PSEM) Group. I am also a member of the Planetary Space Environment Laboratory at the HKU Shenzhen Institute of Research and Innovation. I received my PhD in Geophysics in 2021 from the University of Science and Technology of China, where I conducted my doctoral research in the Ionosphere Lab. I was a research visitor at the High Altitude Observatory, National Center for Atmospheric Research.

My research combines global numerical models, including GAMERA and TIE-GCM, with observations to investigate magnetic reconnection, ionospheric electrodynamics, and magnetosphere–ionosphere coupling at Earth and Jupiter. I have authored over 30 papers, including 14 as first or corresponding author. I have secured RGC GRF and Guangdong NSF funding as PI. I teach three undergraduate courses at HKU. I have organized scientific sessions at AOGS and COSPAR, am a member of the COSPAR ISWAT H4-03 action team, and serve as a reviewer for journals including GRL, JGR, Science China Earth Sciences, and Radio Science.

01

Research

Global numerical modeling of planetary space environments and space weather, supported by observational analysis.

MAGNETOSPHERES

Magnetospheric dynamics

Magnetic topology, reconnection, and solar-wind interactions in planetary magnetospheres, including Jupiter and Ganymede, with ongoing work on Jovian turbulence.

IONOSPHERES

Ionospheric electrodynamics

How neutral winds and conductivity shape electric fields and plasma drifts, from daily variations to space weather responses.

COUPLED SYSTEMS

Magnetosphere–ionosphere coupling

How rotation, winds, and magnetic topology couple magnetospheres, ionospheres, and thermospheres across planets. Planned work includes modeling Jupiter’s thermosphere.
