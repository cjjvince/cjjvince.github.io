SPACE PHYSICS · PLANETARY SCIENCE

Understanding planetary
space environments.

I study how planetary magnetospheres and upper atmospheres interact, from Earth to Jupiter.

I am a Research Assistant Professor at The University of Hong Kong and a member of Prof. Binzheng Zhang’s Planetary Space Environment Modeling group. My work combines global numerical simulations with observations to investigate magnetic reconnection, ionospheric electrodynamics, and magnetosphere–ionosphere coupling.

I received my PhD in Geophysics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2021. Before joining HKU, I was a research visitor at the High Altitude Observatory, National Center for Atmospheric Research.

01

Research

JUPITER & ITS MOONS

Magnetic dynamics
across scales

Magnetic-field dynamics in Jupiter’s magnetosphere, from global topology and reconnection to fluctuations and turbulence, alongside plasma interactions at Ganymede.

EARTH’S UPPER ATMOSPHERE

Ionospheric
electrodynamics

Electric fields, plasma drifts, and the response of the coupled ionosphere–thermosphere system to solar and geomagnetic forcing.

COMPARATIVE PLANETOLOGY

Coupling across
planetary systems

Comparative magnetosphere–ionosphere–thermosphere coupling: how planetary rotation, neutral winds, and magnetic topology shape electrodynamics and auroral processes at Earth and giant planets.

Methods: global numerical modelling, magnetohydrodynamic simulations, and observational data analysis.
